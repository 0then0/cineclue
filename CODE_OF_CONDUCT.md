# Code of Conduct

CineClue welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Discuss code and ideas respectfully, give constructive feedback and accept good-faith corrections.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, disclosure of private information and unwanted sexual content are not acceptable. These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.

## Expected behavior

- Critique the work and explain your reasoning; do not attack the person.
- Respect different experience levels, cultures and accessibility needs.
- Keep discussions relevant and respect requests to stop unwanted contact.
- Correct mistakes constructively and give credit for others' contributions.

## Reporting

Contact the repository maintainer, [0then0](https://github.com/0then0), through a private contact
method listed on their profile, if one is available. Include relevant links and context, and avoid
publishing another person's private information. If no private contact is listed, open an issue
requesting a private contact channel without naming affected people or describing sensitive details.
If a report concerns a maintainer, contact another uninvolved maintainer when available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove harmful content, restrict participation or ban participants for serious or repeated violations. Responses should be proportionate and explained privately when practical. Maintainers are held to the same standards.

Reports are handled discreetly. Information is shared only as needed to investigate and respond;
absolute confidentiality cannot be guaranteed. Retaliation against someone for a good-faith report
is itself a violation. Anyone involved in an incident should not decide its outcome when an
uninvolved maintainer is available.
